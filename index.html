About

I am Yinhe (Terry) Zhou, CFO at ONEAIX Technologies. My work spans financial management, fundraising, and cross-border investment, with experience across China and international markets.

Previously, I worked at Silicon Valley Bank on capital markets development and venture debt for technology companies. I led financing and cross-border capital structuring projects for more than 100 startups, including companies in artificial intelligence and enterprise services, and helped introduce innovative venture debt structures to the Chinese market.

I also led regional investment and financing business development at Akulaku, where I built a team and operating processes for a new business from the ground up. Earlier in my career, I gained experience at China International Capital Corporation (CICC) and Deloitte. Across these roles, my focus has been on supporting business growth while managing financial risk.
